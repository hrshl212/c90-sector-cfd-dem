# Contributing

Contributions must preserve the clean-room publication boundary.

Acceptable contributions include corrections to the independent narrative,
sanitized derived datasets, original diagrams, and public-only analysis or
validation scripts.

Do not submit:

- MFiX or MFiX-Exa source code;
- modifications, patches, diffs, or source-derived pseudocode;
- private input decks, executables, checkpoints, plots, or raw logs;
- cluster paths, account information, scheduler identifiers, or internal
  build/run metadata; or
- third-party content without documented redistribution rights.

Proposed quantitative changes must state units, normalization, measurement
window, and validation scope. Focused or one-step evidence must not be
presented as sustained coupled or scaling qualification.

By contributing original material to this repository, you agree that it may
be distributed under the repository's MIT license.
